# Notably security and privacy
Last updated: 2026-10-03
Canonical URL: https://notably.no/personvern

## Controller
- ARTI CONSULT AS (org. no. 929 098 609 MVA), C. Sundts gate 55, 5004 Bergen, Norway, operates Notably.
- When an organisation uses Notably to process meeting content on its behalf, the organisation is normally the controller and Notably the processor.
- Sales contact: when contacting businesses about Notably, ARTI processes role-based contact details (name, title, work email, phone) from public sources such as the Brønnøysund Register Centre and company websites, plus the dialogue. Opening offers, agreement documents, the IT document or the booking page from links we sent is recorded (when and how often); IP addresses are not stored. Legal basis: legitimate interest. Opt out by replying or writing to support@notably.no. Contact details are kept as long as the business is relevant to us as a potential customer; if you ask us to stop contacting you, we delete or block your details.

## Security and privacy claims shown on website
- GDPR-compatible workflows.
- Core customer content systems self-hosted in Germany.
- TLS encryption in transit, access controls and workspace separation.
- No model training on customer data.

## Hosting and data location
- Web app, API, workers, PostgreSQL database, authentication and self-hosted Supabase software run on Hetzner infrastructure in Nuremberg, Germany.
- Private meeting recording objects are stored in Hetzner-managed object storage in Falkenstein, Germany.
- Supabase Inc. does not host Notably's core database or authentication service.
- Application email is sent from Notably's own infrastructure at Hetzner in Germany. All customer content (meetings, recordings, transcripts, summaries and email sent from Notably) is stored and processed in the EU/EEA. The exceptions do not concern meeting content: payments (Stripe), mobile push notifications (which contain no meeting titles), sign-in with Google, Microsoft or Apple if the user chooses it, optional analytics, and AI tools the customer connects themselves (for example Claude or ChatGPT via MCP). Such processing outside the EU/EEA relies on a relevant legal transfer mechanism (EU–US Data Privacy Framework or EU Standard Contractual Clauses). Provider support, security and moderation staff at ElevenLabs and Microsoft may in limited cases access data from outside the EU; this is governed by data processing agreements and the EU Standard Contractual Clauses. Technical operating logs without meeting content at Recall's subprocessors may be held in the EU or the US.

## Optional analytics and marketing
- Optional analytics and marketing tools stay off until allowed through Personvernvalg (privacy choices).
- If allowed, the public website loads Google Tag Manager, Google Analytics with conversion measurement, Meta Pixel and TikTok Pixel. Nothing loads before consent, and Google Consent Mode defaults to denied.
- Consent can be withdrawn at any time from Personvernvalg in the footer or on the privacy page; withdrawal sends revoke signals and deletes known marketing cookies.
